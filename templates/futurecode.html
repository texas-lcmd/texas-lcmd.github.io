Our Legislative Initiatives

Medical Debt Relief Legislation

Support comprehensive bills that provide relief to those burdened by medical debt, including protections against aggressive collection practices.

Healthcare Access Expansion

Advocate for policies that expand healthcare coverage and reduce the number of uninsured Texans, a key driver of medical debt.

Transparency in Medical Billing

Promote legislation that requires clear and upfront pricing for medical services to help patients make informed decisions and avoid unexpected bills.
