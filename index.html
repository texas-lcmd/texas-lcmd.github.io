Longhorns Crushing Medical Debt

We Crush Medical Debt

Our mission is to ensure that a medical crisis should not be followed by a financial crisis. We assist patients in need in eliminating their medical debt through hospital charity care policies.

Partnerships

Our partners are integral to our mission. They provide us with the space to connect Austin locals to Charity Care, empowering our community and driving positive change.

Micah 6

Micah 6 Food Pantry helps low income individuals and families meet basic needs for food and sustenance. They are open to distribute foods on Thursday evenings and Saturday mornings.

CommUnity Care

CommUnity Care's Southeast Health and Wellness Center is part of Austin's largest safety net primary care system. They work in conjunction with Central Health's MAP Program and are open Monday to Saturday.

Street Forum

Street Forum is a mutual aid organization that works to distribute resources to the unhoused community. They hold space at Republic Square Park every week on Sunday and distribute supplies to anyone who needs them.

Sunrise Homeless Navigation Center

Sunrise Homeless Navigation Center is the largest provider of homeless services in Travis County. They offer pathways to housing for people experiencing homelessness and are open Monday to Friday.

RAISE Texas

RAISE Texas is a nonprofit coalition that advocates for state-level policy changes to promote financial security and economic mobility for Texans. In partnership with LCMD, we advocate for stronger hospital financial assistance policies and expanded access to medical debt relief across the state.
