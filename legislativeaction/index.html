Why Legislative Action Matters

Medical debt is a critical issue affecting millions of Americans. It's the leading cause of personal bankruptcy and impacts access to healthcare, housing, and financial stability.

Through strategic legislative advocacy, we can create systemic change that protects consumers, holds healthcare providers accountable, and builds a more equitable healthcare system.
